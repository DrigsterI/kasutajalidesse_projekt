## Project idea
Create a convenient and modern movie portal that combines ticket purchase and social interaction

## Descrition
This is a modern cinema portal where you can quickly pick a movie and book your tickets. Beside just browsing film details, you can check ratings from various sources and leave your own reviews to share your experience.
